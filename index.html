What apartment types (1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK) are available at E2 Homes Raipur?

We offer fully furnished 1 BHK Executive Apartments (ideal for 1-3 guests), 2 BHK Deluxe Family Suites (ideal for 4-6 guests), and 3 BHK Royal Grand Suites (ideal for 6-10 guests or group stays) at Empresia Elite near Kamal Vihar. All units feature air-conditioned bedrooms, living lounges, private washrooms, and fully equipped kitchens.

Where is E2 Homes Raipur located?

E2 Homes Raipur is located at Empresia Elite, Near Kamal Vihar Sector 8A, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 492001. It is situated in a premium gated residential tower offering high security, peaceful surrounding, and excellent road connectivity.

Is the homestay convenient for visiting Shri Venkatesh Superspeciality Hospital, VY Hospital, and other medical centers?

Yes! E2 Homes Raipur provides easy, rapid access to Shri Venkatesh Superspeciality Hospital, VY Hospital, MMI Narayana Hospital, Ramkrishna CARE Hospital, Suyash Hospital, and Balco Medical Centre via major ring roads and expressways.

Yes, absolutely! Each suite features a fully functional kitchen equipped with a gas stove, refrigerator, RO water purifier, basic cookware, and dining utensils. This is especially beneficial for medical recovery diets and families.

Is vehicle parking available at the property?

Yes, covered and open designated parking slots inside the gated Empresia Elite residential complex are provided free of cost to all checked-in guests.
